This page tracks the public experiments that are shaping my current work.

## Creator signal

The creator experiment asks how a personal brand, niche content, and an email
list can work together without turning into a content treadmill.

The first artifact is the [Creator Signal Audit](/creator-signal-audit/).

## Wraith Reach

Wraith Reach is the software product idea behind the creator experiment.

The short version: help creators understand what their content is signaling,
which audience behaviors matter, and how to turn platform attention into owned
relationships.

## Nokmetia

Nokmetia is the skincare and body-care project. The current direction is a
small-batch sales model where a drop only moves forward when enough people
commit.

## Email system

The email system is the glue. Public pages collect interest. The dynamic mail
management app handles lists, consent, campaigns, open tracking, click tracking,
and agent-assisted campaign work.
