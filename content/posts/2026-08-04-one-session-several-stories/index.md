I have footage. The harder question is what each piece of it is supposed to say.

My video plan kept pulling skating, strength training, filmmaking and technical work into one upload. They all interest me, but someone who came for skating may have little reason to watch an explanation of my editing tools.

### Return to a baseline

For the personal channel, I wanted to revisit an older flow-skating video and ask whether I had become a better skater or simply a stronger, more confident one.

That calls for comparable movements and camera angles, complete attempts and an account of what still feels weak. Strength training can appear where it helps explain the skating. I still needed to film the new attempts before claiming progress.

### Decide what each edit offers

A readable movement clip, a short story about an attempt and a useful observation each need their own opening and payoff. Reusing the footage leaves those editing decisions to be made.

The proposed rhythm was one long personal video every two weeks, with a few Shorts between episodes. Creator-workflow breakdowns could follow once I had several episodes to discuss.

That is enough to start filming. I can save the next manifesto until I have finished an edit.
