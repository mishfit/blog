I pushed back on a Wraith Reach script because the opening took too long to reach the reason for the video.

The frustration was easy to state. Algorithms can feel rigged, and going viral may bring viewers who have little interest in the work. I could introduce my engineering background briefly, then show what this video would help someone understand.

The revision put that opening into about twenty seconds and moved into an example.

### Give the background a purpose

I have published mobile apps and built distributed systems. That experience explains why I am building the tool. Audience-growth results would need their own evidence.

The example starts with a person asking a relevant question. An existing video may contain an answer, and the proposed workflow connects that question to a useful clip. That is something a viewer can follow before hearing about the time it might save.

The product is being built toward that workflow. Concept graphics and working demonstrations need to be recognizable for what they show.

I also cut the second introduction, where the script restated the frustration after already promising an answer. Reducing the manual walkthrough helped keep the question-to-clip idea visible.

I have no viewer-performance result for this revision. What changed was the script's order and the time it took to begin the explanation.
