An interesting patch of light at home gave me a three-photo post to finish. I chose the images, reviewed crops and background blur, and wrote a caption about the light that had caught my attention.

I wanted the person in the photos preserved, without generative replacement or face and body retouching. Comparing crop-only and blurred versions helped me decide on the edit. The three angles also gave people a question to answer.

### Check where it appeared

The set was published on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mishochu/p/DfSubYaEvnk/), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/post/Ugkx5fsHGLEQ5e-o23TIR1ItHX_KcXuqZohm) and [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@mishochu/video/7704042318118112543). The posting session recorded each result.

Snapchat's upload flow appeared to accept the submission, but the Story could not be confirmed. I needed to check the phone before deciding whether another upload was necessary.

The photos and captions were saved in dated publishing folders. I could recover the files and exact wording later without downloading compressed copies from a platform.

Finishing this post gave me a completed piece of work within the time I had. There was no need to turn it into a long video first.
