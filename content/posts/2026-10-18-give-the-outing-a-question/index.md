Car talk, a workout and a street skate can fill an episode without giving someone a reason to wonder what happens next. While reviewing my next video, I wanted a question that would connect those activities.

Could I finish the workout and still skate a connected line smoothly? Could I make the exit from a turn look deliberate? Either question gives me something to establish at the start and return to after practice.

### Show the adjustment

I want to record an initial attempt, explain what I noticed and try one adjustment. Returning to the same camera angle should make the comparison readable, even if the second attempt is disappointing.

The setup is solo, with an X4 and a HoverAir Pro and no phone. A video made by a crew can suggest shots, but I still have to place and operate my own cameras. Fixed passing shots and selected follow footage can add variety around a full-body view of the attempt.

I also want uninterrupted movement and wheel sound. A confident prediction followed by a wobble might supply a joke. The clean skating and sincere reactions need room too.

This is a shooting plan. Recording the decisions between activities should give me something to edit beyond the activities themselves.
