While planning videos for Wraith Reach and Nokmetia, I kept finding footage that could serve both. The reason to watch still needed to fit each audience.

For Wraith, the proposed story was about finding relevant conversations and bringing useful content to them. For Nokmetia, the outing could lead into a repeatable grooming routine, with product explanations available for interested viewers. Someone watching the skating might simply want to enjoy the outing.

Those choices affect the opening and the edit. A different logo would leave the underlying story unchanged.

### Budget for the original edits

The proposed twelve-week library contained eighteen long videos and fifty-four original short edits. Reusing some of them across platforms increased the number of placements. Each placement still needed an export and caption.

Keeping those counts separate helped me think about the workload. The schedule was a proposal. I could extend it if the editing took longer, without pretending that an export was another original story.

I also needed to leave room for what the work taught me. A title about a month of content would require a month of observations. A demonstration would need to show working behavior. The footage and response would have to supply the next story.
