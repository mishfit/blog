I use email as the durable thread between scattered public experiments.

Social posts are useful for discovery. Email is where the thinking can become
more direct, less performative, and easier to find again.

## What I send

Expect notes about:

- software I am building
- creator and audience experiments
- Nokware and Nokmetia work
- lessons from things that worked
- lessons from things that looked promising and then bit the dust

The pattern is one idea per email: a short story or observation, the useful
lesson, and a next step if there is one.
