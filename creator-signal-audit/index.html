Creator Signal Audit

The Creator Signal Audit is a small checklist for figuring out what your next nine posts are teaching the algorithm, your audience, and yourself.

Most creator advice starts too late. It talks about hooks, edits, captions, or posting frequency before answering the quieter question:

What is this account actually training people to expect?

What the audit looks at

The audit is built around five signals:

  1. Topic clarity
  2. Repeated audience problem
  3. Format pattern
  4. Proof that strangers care
  5. A path from attention to an owned list

It is the first step in the larger Wraith Reach idea: use content data to find the niche, understand active followers, and move the right people into an owned relationship.
